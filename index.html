A little about me

I am a trilingual Costume Designer and Art Historian with specialist expertise in costume design, supervision, and historical research. Currently serving as Costume Supervisor at Thorpe Park by Merlin Entertainments, I lead the Costume Department in the concept development, design, creation, and maintenance of costumes for the park’s attractions, live shows, and special events. In 2025, I successfully directed the costume delivery for Europe’s top theme park scare event Fright Nights, designing and overseeing costumes for eight seasonal mazes and live performances, dressing and managing a cast of over 300 performers. Skilled in both digital and traditional design methods, I combine proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite and Procreate with strong illustration and freehand drawing skills. With a Master’s degree in Historical Costume (Distinction) from Arts University Bournemouth and a Bachelor’s in Fine Art from the University of Hong Kong, I bring a unique combination of artistic vision, practical costume construction skills, and event production experience.
